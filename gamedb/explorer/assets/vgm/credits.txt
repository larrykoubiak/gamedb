Explorer soundtrack playback

The emulation engine is ZXTune Web by hhungry2, based on ZXTune by
Vitamin/CAIG. It includes the third-party decoders listed in the
component notices and corresponding source.

Component notices
/vgm/zxtune-f59403a-2/licenses.txt

Corresponding source

https://github.com/hhungry2/zxtune-web/archive/f59403aa92cd798cadc3a8de4b5ea3f174659614.tar.gz

Build used for Explorer

GameDB exposes all 256 HES track indices. After checking out
f59403aa92cd798cadc3a8de4b5ea3f174659614, apply this change in the ZXTune
directory.

  sed -i 's/TOTAL_TRACKS_COUNT = 32;/TOTAL_TRACKS_COUNT = 256;/' src/formats/multitrack/hes.cpp

Compile with Emscripten 4.0.21. The Explorer build compiles the
3rdparty/ht/Core/c68k/c68kexec.c object with -O0.

  make platform=emscripten -C apps/zxtune-web

Copy bin/emscripten/release/zxtune.mjs and zxtune.wasm into Explorer's
vgm/zxtune-f59403a-2 directory. Explorer loads the decoder as a separate
module and works with an interface-compatible replacement.

Sega Saturn SSF playback

The SSF player uses ssfplay_tools by Niek Vlessert, based on Mednafen.
It is available under the GNU GPL, version 2 or later.
/vgm/ssfplay-a28e0d7/license.txt

Corresponding source
https://github.com/niekvlessert/ssfplay_tools/tree/a28e0d7e14a3dc04a486ea26f30bc52edad3dc4d
https://github.com/niekvlessert/ssfplay_tools/archive/a28e0d7e14a3dc04a486ea26f30bc52edad3dc4d.tar.gz

In the upstream CMakeLists.txt, add "SHELL:-sEXPORT_ES6=1" after
"SHELL:-sMODULARIZE=1" in the ssfplay_wasm linker options. Export the
additional functions _ssfplay_length_ms, _ssfplay_fade_ms, and _ssfplay_reset,
and set the ssfplay_wasm output suffix to ".mjs". Build with Emscripten 4.0.21.

  emcmake cmake -S . -B build-wasm -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release -DSSFPLAY_BUILD_CLI=OFF -DSSFPLAY_BUILD_WASM=ON -DSSFPLAY_BUILD_LIBVGM_AB_PLAYER=OFF -DBUILD_TESTING=OFF
  cmake --build build-wasm

Copy ssfplay.mjs and ssfplay.wasm to Explorer's vgm/ssfplay-a28e0d7
directory. Explorer loads this decoder in the audio worker for SSF tracks.

Streamed audio playback

The Linux Explorer image uses the vgmstream CLI r2168 to prepare FLAC
copies of streamed tracks such as Nintendo AFC. The vgmstream contributors
distribute the decoder under the license below.
