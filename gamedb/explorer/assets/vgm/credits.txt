Explorer soundtrack playback

The emulation engine is ZXTune Web by hhungry2, based on ZXTune by
Vitamin/CAIG. It includes the third-party decoders listed in the
component notices and corresponding source.

Build used for Explorer

GameDB disabled the Saturn and Dreamcast decoder and exposed all 256 HES
track indices. After checking out f59403aa92cd798cadc3a8de4b5ea3f174659614,
apply these source changes in the ZXTune directory.

  sed -i 's/ gme he ht hvl / gme he hvl /' apps/zxtune-web/Makefile
  sed -i '/RegisterSDSFSupport/d' src/core/plugins/players/plugins_list.cpp
  sed -i 's/TOTAL_TRACKS_COUNT = 32;/TOTAL_TRACKS_COUNT = 256;/' src/formats/multitrack/hes.cpp

Compile with Emscripten 4.0.21.

  make platform=emscripten -C apps/zxtune-web

Copy bin/emscripten/release/zxtune.mjs and zxtune.wasm into Explorer's
vgm/zxtune-f59403a-2 directory. Explorer loads the decoder as a separate
module and works with an interface-compatible replacement.
